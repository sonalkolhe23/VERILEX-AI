Classification evidence

  • Relevance class: Direct AI relevance · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Official Regulation (EU) 2024/1689: Article 1 is horizontal, Article 5 prohibits practices, Article 6 classifies high-risk systems and Annex III spans eight domains. — The cited provisions make AI the central subject.
  • Sector tags: General cross sector · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Official Regulation (EU) 2024/1689: Article 1 is horizontal, Article 5 prohibits practices, Article 6 classifies high-risk systems and Annex III spans eight domains. — The cited provisions substantiate the retained cross-sector classification.
  • Sector tags: Critical infrastructure · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Original Official Journal text, Annex III, point 2: critical infrastructure. — Explicitly addresses AI safety components in managing and operating critical digital infrastructure and essential-service supplies. This is a subject classification, not an organisation-specific applicability finding.
  • Sector tags: Transport and mobility · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Original Official Journal text, Annex III, point 2: management and operation of road traffic. — Road-traffic AI safety components are a substantive transport application expressly specified in the annex.
  • Sector tags: Education · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Original Official Journal text, Annex III, point 3(a)-(d): education and vocational training. — Specifies AI uses in educational admission, learning-outcome evaluation, educational placement and monitoring prohibited behaviour during tests.
  • Sector tags: Employment and labour · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Original Official Journal text, Annex III, point 4(a)-(b): employment, workers management and access to self-employment. — Specifies AI applications in recruitment, employment decisions, task allocation and worker monitoring.
  • Sector tags: Health · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Original Official Journal text, Annex III, points 5(a), 5(c) and 5(d): healthcare services, health insurance and emergency healthcare triage. — Contains express health-related AI provisions, including healthcare eligibility, health-insurance risk assessment and emergency triage.
  • Sector tags: Financial services · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Original Official Journal text, Annex III, points 5(b)-(c): creditworthiness and credit scores; risk assessment and pricing for life and health insurance. — Contains explicit AI applications in credit and insurance. The label does not remove the exceptions or conditions in the cited provisions.
  • Sector tags: Law enforcement · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Original Official Journal text, Annex III, point 6(a)-(e): law enforcement. — Specifies law-enforcement AI uses, including evidence evaluation and profiling during criminal detection, investigation or prosecution.
  • Sector tags: Migration asylum and border management · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Original Official Journal text, Annex III, point 7(a)-(d): migration, asylum and border control management. — Expressly addresses AI applications in migration risk assessment, asylum and visa examination, and border-management identification.
  • Sector tags: Justice · Publications Office of the European Union / EUR-Lex · Original Official Journal text, Annex III, point 8(a): assistance to judicial authorities and similar use in alternative dispute resolution. — Judicial assistance in researching and interpreting facts and law and applying law to concrete facts is an explicit AI application.
